## Notes on the method

- **The arms:** built at the tag's code (a worktree of cfbe62b, with the margin patch of M7-01's spikes).
  - cpu_m05 is `chart --takes 4` on a hard-linked copy of each kept folder; the GPU arms are the same config with the
    GPU takes swapped in.
  - The route check is identical note for note on 23 of 23 songs, and the kept folders were unchanged.
  - The spike scores, L1 and D1 are scored with the take-score spike's onset-splice evaluator, which reproduces the
    built m05 within 0.008 on every song.
- **Many GPU charts are the CPU ones:** the GPU take of seed 20260609 is byte-identical to the CPU kept take on 8 of 23
  songs. GPU m05 equals cpu_m05 on break and take_me_out. On those songs GPU and CPU agree by construction.
- **Part C's variants:** 207 variant builds (V1-V3) on the m05, cpu_m05 and CPU one configs. Onsets and lanes are
  identical across the variants; a no-change build reproduced part B's chart byte for byte.
- **Not run:** torchcrepe (C-f), because a throwaway install was not confirmed.
- **The picks assert on s09 and s11:** M1's build stopped on its own picks assert on two songs. On m0, one window each
  differed from its recomputed picks, a near-tie hidden by the 4-place rounding of the recorded scores. The built picks
  stand, both songs were rebuilt complete, and the difference is recorded.
- **Scripts:** eval/m8/m802/ (scripts/ for the intake, A and B; C/scripts and D/scripts for C and D). They ran on the
  dev machine. Their paths are placeholders ($DEV, `<shm>`, `<wt>`, `<checkout>`, `<scratchpad>`): fill them in to
  rerun.

### How it was computed (the choices taken)

- **Variant arms** (C/scripts/build_c.py), built as M7-01 part A did, with each HOPO mode paired with one floor cap. Our onsets and lanes are identical
  across the variants on every song, arm and chart (0 mismatches, asserted), so per-note rules are exact recombinations.
  - V1: hopo "source" + cap 300 ms.
  - V2: "natural" + 400 ms.
  - V3: "forced" (today) + 500 ms.

  Each is `songcharter assemble` of part B's config with only the guitar part's `hopo` changed, in a copy of the tag's code
  (<shm>/m8/m2/code). The copy carries one uncommitted patch: `quantize.sustain_kept` keeps an Expert or Hard sustain when it is at least 200 ms
  and (at least 0.75 beat or at least the cap). The cap comes from `SC_M8_CAP`, and with the variable unset the code is unchanged. A V0 build (no change)
  of creep's GPU m05 reproduced part B's notes.chart byte for byte. The cap also reaches the bass; only the guitar is measured here.
  The 207 builds ran through tools/heavy.sh, one song per job.
- **Alignment** (C/scripts/align.py): score.py's own function, run in process. It gives the lag of our song.ogg against each chart's input.wav and the
  chart's delay and span. Our F1@50 and F1@100 equal part B's run_score to 4 decimals (16 of 16 arm and chart pairs checked).
  **Caveat:** 25 of the 67 charts (all alternatives) align badly by score.py's own test: 4.5 or fewer of 9 windows at ncc 0.3 or more, or a lag MAD of 2 ms or more.
  They are on sooth and s01, s03-s12. The order's rule (every chart) is followed. A rerun on the well-aligned charts
  only (C/C_aligned.json, C/tables_aligned.md) leaves every verdict as it is: natural +0.018 [-0.006, +0.048]; open rules' exact -0.003 to -0.005; cap 300 slow -0.002.
- **Definitions:**
  - Engine forced strum: source strums a note that natural HOPOs (forced HOPO the reverse).
  - R1: source on songs whose grid octave is not 1 (pm2, troop, s01, s10 at 2; sooth, s02, s04 at 0.5), else today.
  - R2, as the order words it: today with the engine's forced strums set to HOPO inside fast runs (8 or more onsets, each at most
    125 ms apart). M7-01's R2 (everywhere) is beside it.
  - T1: taps become HOPOs. T2: taps outside fast runs become HOPOs.
  - Floor-drop share: each human sustain judged against max(200 ms, min(0.75 beat of our grid, cap)).
  - o2: an open takes the lowest lane of the nearest fretted onset before it, else after it.
  - o3: opens kept only in runs of 3 or more consecutive open onsets.
  - Open precision and recall: a human (our) open within 50 ms.
  - C-e precision: a removed repeat with no human note within 50 ms in any chart.
  - Human notes removed: the share of a chart's notes whose 50 ms match is a removed repeat, mean over charts, and the most for any song.
  - Stem test: librosa onset strength peak over [t, t + 60 ms] on the kept guitar stem, under the 25th or 50th percentile of our onsets.
- **C-f** (C/scripts/cf_contour.py) imports main's eval/m8/contour (pitch.py, analyze.py) read-only and runs it on GPU m05's
  onsets in the primary's frame, with the kept guitar stems: pYIN, a CQT peak, and a harmonic-summed CQT peak. 10 songs have fast runs. **torchcrepe was
  not run.** pyin_step1 misses the bar, but this measurer cannot confirm the owner's allowance for the throwaway install, so it
  stays not run.
- "Without humans under 0.6" uses the humans' own agreement on that measure (alternative chart against the primary): states, sustain
  agreement, exact lanes, contour. On states and sustains no song is under 0.6. On exact lanes most are, which is why C-d's third column is small.

  delay is the chart's. A check against part B's run_score: F1@50 and F1@100 are identical to 4 decimals on every arm and chart tried
  (all_the_small_things, creep, break; 16 of 16).
- Windows: score.py's window_scores over each chart's span, 30 s, with matches at 100 ms counted in the human onset's
  window. The last window can be shorter, and its rate uses its real length.
- **A fast window (the choice the order left):** the human chart plays 8 or more notes a second over the window (its
  onsets divided by the window's length): "rate8". The variant "peak8" counts a window with any 1-s stretch of 8 or more human onsets.
  It is looser: 19 songs qualify, and GPU m05's recall there is 0.730 song mean, 0.713 note-pooled.
- A song's recall is the mean over its charts of (matched / human notes) summed over that chart's fast windows.
  Pooled is the mean over the songs that have fast windows. "Note-pooled" sums the notes over every song and chart.
  CIs come from a paired bootstrap over songs (10,000 resamples, seed 7).
- sooth, pm2 and troop all have rate8 windows. The other songs with such windows are creep, s01, s06, s07, s08, s09 and s10.

### Caveats

- The sample is 10 songs (rate8), and three of them have only 1 or 4 fast windows (creep, s01, sooth), so the CIs
  span about ±0.04. M5-99's 47 % on sooth used another measure (human notes at 8-12 a second on the owner's own chart). It is not
  comparable to this window recall, which is 0.704 for sooth's GPU m05.
- Recall is measured at 100 ms. Fewer notes is the main cause (ratio about 0.7). Timing is not checked here beyond F1@100.
- The verdict moves to just over 0.7 when badly aligned alternative charts are dropped. The order's rule (every chart)
  gives 0.659, and that is the reading this report goes by.
