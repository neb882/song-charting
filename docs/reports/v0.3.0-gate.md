## The advisor's report

Read: CLAUDE.md, PROGRESS (M6), PIPELINE's gates, the code diff v0.2.1...HEAD (songcharter/, packaging/, eval/),
M6-06 and M6-10's reports, set20-v03.md, DESIGN 2.3/2.5/2.6/2.10, the guide. Measured from the dev machine's run
folders with scripts that print numbers only (no titles, no charts opened). Findings, most severe first.

1. **The GPU path is unmeasured where it now matters (judgement; the owner's PC check can close it).** M6-10 makes
   allin1 fold 0 the model on CUDA, and since M6-03/05/06 allin1's beats and downbeats set the grid: the bar phase
   (`with_downbeats`), the doubled grid's start (`allin1_start`), the halving test. Every gate number is from fold 0 on
   the CPU; M6-10 says "nothing ran on a GPU". CUDA inference can move allin1's frames, and the three rules are
   robust to small shifts (medians, a vote within a quarter beat, a start refined onto the drums), but no one has seen
   it. Ask the owner's PC check to compare the test song's `report.json` grid record with the CPU golden run's: octave 1,
   142.0 BPM, `octave_stats.halving` ratio 0.994 against allin1's 142.9 BPM, `downbeat_source` allin1 (the golden
   record on this machine), and, if he can, one slow song (see 3) and one doubled song.

2. **The friend's test song will change on his GPU (judgement, expected).** On CUDA v0.2.1 ran the 8-fold ensemble;
   v0.3.0 runs fold 0. `pipeline.py`'s docstring: with fold 0, "labels" covers all of Solo 2 and the first half of
   Solo 1 and adds one span nobody confirmed; the ensemble covered Solo 2 only. A rechart of his test song gains a
   solo tracker he did not confirm, and the sections cache key changes, so allin1 reruns once a song. M6-10 says Q29 is
   answered "with the release": the owner should approve it knowingly and expect the solo difference in his check.

3. **Halved songs lose short sustains (measured; known, deferred in M5-99, but the friend will meet it).** The 20-song
   run on b80937c halved 2 of the owner's 13 picks (73.0 and 73.1 BPM; the stress shred song 77.0 makes 3 in 23 songs).
   At 73 BPM the 0.75-beat floor is 616 ms. One halved song: guitar sustain share 0.12 against the human's 0.10,
   fine; the other: 0.04 against 0.25 (bass 0.13 against 0.27), 199 and 133 Expert sustains dropped. Not a blocker;
   worth a line in the owner's notes, and M5-99's item stands.

4. **The halving rule's exposure (judgement; evidence favours it).** `halving_test(160, allin1 at 80)` is True
   (reproduced on a copy): a 150-170 BPM song allin1 reads in half time is halved, and the evidence guard cannot
   refuse it (the halved beats are a subset of the first's). Against that: 23 songs, 3 halvings, each on a song whose
   beat F1 against the human exceeds the half-rate ceiling (0.955, 0.985, 0.744), every other ratio 0.99-1.02. The
   friend has no control (the UI and CLI expose neither `grid_octave` nor a bpm hint), only the "tempo halved ...
   check sync" warning the page shows. Acceptable for v0.3.0; DESIGN 2.3's "one positive in eleven" could cite the two
   slow songs.

5. **A bpm hint does not stop the halving (code, low, dev-only).** `_octave_pass` runs `_halving_pass` whenever the
   density test says 1 under auto, so `AssembleConfig.bpm` 160 is halved when allin1 says 80. Unreachable from the
   UI or CLI; untested.

6. **Claims against code: they match.** `HALVE_TOLERANCE` 0.1, 16 beats, the 85 floor, 0.7 evidence; `DOWNBEAT_SNAP`
   0.25; `RUN_CHORD_S` 0.120; star power `max_bpm` 160; `DOUBLED_BASS_SLOTS` (2, 0); `AUTO_MODEL` fold 0: DESIGN,
   the reports and the docstrings say what the code does. The guide and `install.sh` are unchanged since v0.2.1, so
   the update line, its hash and the guide's install claims stand; CI's update hook covers the tarball path.

7. **The new code's edges hold (reproduced on a copy).** allin1 beats outside the audio, 2 beats, 16 beats,
   duplicates, 0 or 1 beat for `allin1_start`, downbeats before the first or past the last beat, a 1-beat grid: no
   exception, no numpy warning, the documented refusals.

8. **What the gate has not checked:** the update over a real v0.2.1 WSL install (CI does the tarball path only); 4
   CUDA takes with M6-04's run chords (applied per take before the splice: right by design, unmeasured); a halved and
   a doubled song on CUDA (1). The gate logs on the machine show the golden pair with no UNEXPECTED change and the
   stress compare at 0 regressions; the orchestrator's final pair was still running.
