**Choice: torchaudio's English 960 h large bundle with a star before every line** (above 70 % within 250 ms by a
wide margin, so Phase B was built). Pooled accuracy first: best by +0.046 to +0.103. Robustness: best on four of
five probes, within 0.01 on the fifth, no crash. Then install and licence: torchaudio alone (the same for every
torchaudio candidate), MIT weights; its weights are 1.26 GB (the base bundle's 378 MB costs 0.046). Its limits:
English letters only (no romanization), and a collapse on a vocal stem as poor as a full mix, which the confidence
shows (song medians: stems 0.31-0.89, mixes 0.09-0.18; the pipeline warns under 0.25).

## Phase B

- `songcharter/lyrics.py` (new): the text model (lines = phrases, blank lines and [headings] = section gaps, `_` and
  whitespace split words, hyphens split syllables written with `=` so Clone Hero shows the hyphen, the markup the game
  acts on (`" + = # ^ * % $ / § < >`, edge hyphens) left out or changed and counted, punctuation-only tokens shown with
  a neighbouring word, the aligner's spelling: NFKD, lower case, straight apostrophes, `&` as and, numbers spelled);
  `place` (SnapGrid over the syllables' own ticks, a syllable on or before the previous one moved to the next slot,
  phrases at lines and at gaps between syllable starts over REST_SPLIT_S = 3.0 s, phrase_start 0.151 s ahead and
  phrase_end 0.701 s after the last start, snapped, never overlapping); `events`, `read_events`, `shown_lines`.
- The rest threshold from the human charts (S/m4-10/scripts/gaps.py, timings only): 1,076 within-phrase gaps between
  word starts: p50 0.32 s, p95 1.02 s, p99 2.01 s, max 3.02 s; 195 between phrases: p10 0.59 s, p50 1.68 s. At 3.0 s:
  0.09 % of within-phrase gaps would split, 33 % of phrase boundaries are caught. Phrase edges (phrase_edges.py):
  first lyric 0.151 s after phrase_start (median), phrase_end 0.701 s after the last lyric's start (median).
- `songcharter/engines/lyrics.py` (new): `align(text, vocals, out, settings, aligner=None)`, the network in 30 s
  windows with 2 s context on the 20 ms grid, `forced_align` on the CPU, stars before every line, at every section gap
  and at both ends, separators between words, unspelled syllables spread between neighbours. Weights pinned (URL,
  SHA-256 7a889657...43ac7) in models_dir()/lyrics/, SONGCHARTER_OFFLINE honoured, `weights_only=True`. Cache
  out/lyrics.json: key = stem samples + SHA-256 of the text + settings + weights + versions, times by syllable index,
  no text. Confidence: per-word mean character probability, median / p10 / share under 0.05, `low` under 0.25.
  `Unavailable` for missing torch/torchaudio, a torchaudio without the bundle or forced_align, or weights out of
  reach. The network is not kept after a call.
- `chart.py`: `Chart.lyrics` rows, written after the sections on their tick (stable sort); with none, [Events] is
  byte-identical to before. `assemble(config, lyrics=Timed)`: places them after `fit_chart`, report.json `lyrics`
  counts only; the gate: `lyricsReadBack` (notes.chart's lyric events against the written ones) and `noLyrics`

  `lyricsNotChecked` (main's scan.cjs, which the brief's suite command uses, is older until this merges).
- Pipeline: `Options.lyrics` (text or None, checked: a text with no word is an InputError), stage "lyrics" after
