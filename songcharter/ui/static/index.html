              Worth a look

              A time opens its page; Listen here plays the chart from there.

                  Listen

                  The chart as sound over the song, to hear a note that is wrong, late or missing. Guitar and bass: a tone for each note, green lowest to orange highest (an open note lower still), a chord's tones together, HOPOs and taps softer than strums, sustains held. Drums: the kick a thump, the snare a burst, the toms pitched, the cymbals a hiss.
